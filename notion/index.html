FAQ

Do I need a paid Notion plan?

No. The template works on Notion’s free plan. Team sharing and advanced features are subject to Notion’s current plan limits.

How will I receive the template?

After checkout, the Notion duplicate link and getting-started guide are sent automatically by email.

Will you customize it for my business?

No. This product is a ready-made template. Customization and setup services are not included.

What if I do not know how to use it?

Start with the in-template Start Here guide and this FAQ. One-on-one chat support is not provided.

Can multiple people use one purchase?

Each purchase includes one user license. Purchase one license per person for team use.

Are updates included?

A purchase includes the version available at checkout. There is no annual update pass or ongoing update subscription.

Is this for ecommerce sellers?

No. It is designed for freelancers, independent contractors, and project-based work.
